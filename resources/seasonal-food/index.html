Seasonal food

Mooncakes baked here in Greater Sacramento — not premade imports.

Families share mooncakes — 月饼, bánh trung thu — under the harvest moon, at Chinese-forward moon festivals and at Tết Trung Thu. Great Lantern Festival is not hosting its own Elk Grove weekend in 2026; the season still has a table.

This page is for freshly made local mooncakes: pastry baked at neighborhood shops and home kitchens, not boxed imports from a warehouse shelf. Two storefront bakeries and one home kitchen are listed below. Seasonal stock sells through — check with the kitchen before you go.

Mooncake (月饼 / bánh trung thu)

The pastry of the season. Look for cakes baked locally this Mid-Autumn — not premade boxed imports.

  • Pegasus Bakery & Café (storefront) — Hong Kong–style mooncakes — 6825 Stockton Blvd #265, Sacramento, CA 95823
  • ABC Bakery (storefront) — Cantonese Chinese mooncakes — 1309 Florin Rd Ste F, Sacramento, CA 95831
